Agriculture ERP: farm to market

Agriculture, Project Lead, Odoo ERP

Challenge

An agri business planned crop seasons, field work and harvests on paper and ran contract farming, procurement and sales in separate tools, so there was no single view from field to market and no reliable traceability.

What we delivered

  • Set up farms, fields and plots with crop and season details.
  • Built crop season planning with planned inputs such as seed, fertiliser and labour.
  • Used Field Service for field visits and farm tasks recorded on site by field teams.
  • Implemented contract farming workflows so procurement runs against farmer agreements.
  • Recorded harvests per field into inventory with lot-based traceability through to sale and export.
  • Built planned-versus-actual and yield reports by season and field for management.

Outcome

The whole agri operation runs in one system, from season planning and field work to harvest, traceability and sales, with field-level data for better decisions.
